Why Mobile Friendly Websites Matter

If you run a business in Delhi and your website is not mobile friendly, you may be losing potential customers. Many internet users in India access websites from their mobile phones. When someone visits your website on their phone and finds it difficult to use, they may leave quickly and look for another business.

This article explains why mobile friendly websites are essential, how mobile usability affects your business, and what makes a website work well on phones.

The Mobile Reality in India

India has a large mobile internet audience. For many people, smartphones are the primary way they access the internet and search for local businesses.

For businesses in Delhi, this means potential customers may be discovering your business on mobile devices. If your website does not work well on a phone, you can create unnecessary friction for those visitors.

What Is Responsive Design?

Responsive design is an approach to web design that allows a website to adapt to different screen sizes. A responsive website adjusts its layout, images, text, and navigation depending on the device being used.

Mobile Navigation

Navigation on mobile devices is different from desktop navigation. Good mobile navigation should:

  • Use a mobile friendly navigation pattern
  • Keep menu items large enough to tap easily
  • Prioritise the most important pages
  • Provide clear visual feedback
  • Allow users to navigate without unnecessary zooming

Mobile Readability

Text on mobile devices should be readable without requiring visitors to zoom in. Important readability considerations include:

  • Font sizes that are easy to read
  • Sufficient line spacing
  • Good contrast between text and background
  • Content that fits the screen width
  • Paragraphs that are easy to scan

Mobile Performance

Mobile users can be sensitive to slow loading websites. Performance considerations include:

  • Optimised images
  • Minimal use of unnecessarily large files
  • Efficient code
  • Appropriate caching
  • Testing on different mobile devices

Mobile User Experience

Good mobile user experience means visitors can find information and take action without frustration. Important elements include:

  • Clear calls to action that are easy to tap
  • Forms that are simple to complete on phones
  • Phone numbers that can be tapped to call
  • Images that display properly on small screens
  • Simple navigation and scrolling

Mobile Accessibility

Mobile accessibility means making your website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies. Important considerations include:

  • Descriptive alt text for images
  • Proper heading structure
  • Sufficient colour contrast
  • Touch targets that are easy to use
  • Support for screen readers

SEO and Mobile Friendliness

Google primarily uses the mobile version of a website's content for indexing and ranking. A mobile friendly website therefore forms an important part of providing a good search experience.

Local Customers and Mobile Searches

Local business searches often happen when people are looking for information while they are away from their computers. A customer searching for a restaurant, salon, service provider, or shop may visit a website directly from their phone.

Business Enquiries from Mobile

A mobile friendly website can make it easier for visitors to contact your business. When customers can quickly find your phone number, WhatsApp link, email address, location, or contact form, there are fewer obstacles between visiting your website and making an enquiry.

Common Mobile Mistakes

  • Text that is too small to read
  • Buttons that are difficult to tap
  • Content that extends beyond the screen
  • Slow loading pages
  • Pop-ups that unnecessarily block content
  • Forms that are difficult to complete

If your website has these issues, it may be time to consider a website redesign .

Testing Your Website on Mobile

  1. Open your website on your own phone
  2. Check whether the text is readable without zooming
  3. Navigate to your most important pages
  4. Test your contact form
  5. Check how quickly the important pages load
  6. Test buttons, phone links, and WhatsApp links
